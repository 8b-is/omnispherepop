# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be a decent engineer. Assume the person you disagree with is trying to make this project
good, and say what you actually think about the code.

## Expected

- Critique code, designs, and claims. Be specific and show evidence.
- Accept review of your own work without treating it as a verdict on you.
- Prefer the strongest version of an argument you disagree with, then answer it.
- Respect that maintainers are volunteers with limited time; a closed or deferred pull
  request is a decision about scope, not about you.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, or private channels
  connected to the project.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions to relitigate them, or demanding
  unpaid maintainer labour as entitlement.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space the project
runs, including community calls and chat.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to any owner in `OWNERS.toml`. Reports are handled
confidentially. Responses scale with the behaviour: a private word, a public correction,
a temporary block, or a permanent ban from project spaces. Retaliation against someone
who reports in good faith is itself a violation.

Maintainers who violate this document are held to it by the other two owners.
